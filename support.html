Aden Eats Support

For help with your account, an order, a cook application, payments, refunds, or payouts, email support@adeneats.com.

Include in your message

  • The email address associated with your Aden Eats account.
  • Your order number, if the question is about an order.
  • A brief description of what happened and any error message you saw.

Do not email passwords, full card numbers, bank-account numbers, or government identification documents.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion from the account settings in the Aden Eats app. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address associated with your account.
