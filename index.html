Aden Eats Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Aden Eats is operated by Aden Community Ventures LLC. This policy explains the information used by the Aden Eats mobile application and the services that support it.

Information We Collect

  • Account and contact information, including name, email address, phone number, user ID, and profile details.
  • Delivery and pickup information, including physical addresses and precise location when you grant location permission.
  • Order, cart, favorites, reviews, and marketplace activity.
  • Cook application, menu, food-safety, connected-account onboarding status, and earnings information.
  • Photos or videos you choose to upload for profiles, applications, or dishes.
  • Payment and payout information processed by Stripe. Aden Eats does not store full card or bank-account numbers.
  • Device and push-notification identifiers used to deliver order and account updates.
  • Product interaction, diagnostic, crash, and performance information used to operate and improve the service.

How We Use Information

  • Provide accounts, marketplace browsing, ordering, fulfillment, refunds, cook onboarding, and payouts.
  • Calculate service availability and delivery distance.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce marketplace and food-safety requirements, and protect users.
  • Send transactional email and push notifications.
  • Provide support, diagnose failures, measure reliability, and improve Aden Eats.

Sharing and Service Providers

We use service providers as needed to operate Aden Eats, including Supabase for authentication and data hosting, Stripe for payments and connected-account payouts, Expo for application updates and push notifications, Google Maps services for address and location features, and Resend for transactional email. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising.

Visibility and Cook Privacy

Marketplace profiles and dish listings are visible to customers. Cook contact details and private application information are not displayed publicly. A pickup address is disclosed only when required to fulfill a confirmed pickup order.

Retention, Security, and Your Choices

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet applicable recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. We use access controls and encryption supported by our service providers, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

You can control location, photo, camera, and notification permissions in your device settings. You can edit available profile information in the app. To delete your account, use the in-app account deletion control or contact support. Some transaction records may be retained where required.

Children

Aden Eats is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy as Aden Eats changes. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
