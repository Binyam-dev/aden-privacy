Aden Eats Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Aden Eats is operated by Aden Community Ventures. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Aden Eats mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Name and email address
  • Approximate location to show nearby cooks (never stored)
  • Payment information processed securely by Stripe
  • Push notification token for order updates
  • Order history and cook profiles

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect clients with home cooks
  • To process and fulfill orders
  • To send order status notifications
  • To process cook earnings and payouts

Cook Privacy

Clients only see a cook's first name, city, and specialty. Phone numbers, emails, and home addresses are never shown publicly. Pickup addresses are only revealed after an order is confirmed.

Third Party Services

We share data only with: Stripe (payments), Supabase (data storage), Expo (push notifications), Twilio (SMS). We never sell your data.

Food Marketplace Disclaimer

Aden Eats is a technology marketplace connecting clients with independent home cooks. We are not a food producer or restaurant. All meals are prepared by independent cooks in private home kitchens. Aden Community Ventures is not responsible for food quality or safety compliance.
